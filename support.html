Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it.

How do I change my privacy choices?
In the app, go to Settings, then Privacy.

I'm a parent. How do I approve my child's account?
Your child can send you an approval link from the app. If you have trouble, email us.
